# Contributing to SKILL-IACT 🛡️

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **SKILL-IACT**! This project aims to provide AI agents, system integrators, and developers with clear, actionable context and instructions for aligning systems with the **EU AI Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689)**.

---

## How to Contribute 🚀

### 1. Reporting Issues & Requesting Features
If you find an inaccuracy in the legal citations, a bug in the automated scanner, or have a feature request:
* Check the existing issues to see if it has already been reported.
* Open a new issue with a clear description, steps to reproduce, and reference to the relevant EU AI Act Articles if applicable.

### 2. Submitting Pull Requests (PRs)
We welcome contributions that improve the accuracy of our compliance logic, add new languages, or enhance the dashboard UI.
1. **Fork the repository** and clone your fork locally.
2. **Create a feature branch**: `git checkout -b feat/my-awesome-improvement`.
3. **Commit your changes**: Make sure your code complies with our formatting guidelines.
4. **Push to your fork** and submit a PR to the `main` branch of `NicolaCuomo/skill-aiact`.

---

## Guidelines for Legal & Technical Alignment ⚖️

* **Grounded Citations**: Any change to `SKILL.md` or `app.py` regarding risk classification or transparency requirements must reference the official text of **Regulation EU 2024/1689** as published in the Official Journal of the European Union.
* **Agent Portability**: Ensure that updates to the instructions in `SKILL.md` remain under **100 lines** and comply with the **Agent Plugins 1.0.0** specification.
* **UI Consistency**: When updating templates, follow the light Claymorphism theme with round buttons andOutfit/Montserrat typography.
