# AI Act Enforcement Timeline - Post Digital Omnibus (2026)

## 📅 Overview

This document maps all key enforcement dates of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus).

**Current Reference Date:** August 2026  
**Source:** EUR-Lex Consolidated Version (2026-07-27)

---

## ✅ Already Applicable (Past Dates)

### 2 February 2025

| Provision | Description | Status |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Art. 3** | Definitions (AI System, Provider, Deployer, etc.) | ✅ IN FORCE |
| **Art. 5** | Prohibited AI Practices | ✅ IN FORCE |
| **Art. 4** | AI Literacy (as amended by Digital Omnibus) | ✅ IN FORCE |

**Implications for SMEs:**
- Ban on manipulative AI, social scoring, biometric categorisation (with limited exceptions)
- Obligation to develop AI literacy measures for personnel using AI systems
- Definition of what constitutes an "AI system" determines scope of all other obligations

| **Transparency for AI-Generated Content** | Deepfake labeling, chatbot disclosure | ✅ IN FORCE |

**CRITICAL FOR ALL SMEs:**
- **Transparency obligations NOW ACTIVE**: Must inform users when interacting with AI
- **Chatbots must disclose** they are AI systems
- **Deepfakes must be labeled** as artificially generated/manipulated
- **Emotion recognition systems** must inform affected persons
- **Biometric categorisation** requires explicit transparency

**Enforcement Reality:**
- National authorities can now impose sanctions
- Fines up to €15M or 3% global turnover for prohibited practices
- Fines up to €7.5M or 1% for transparency violations

## ⏳ Upcoming Deadlines (Future)

### 2 December 2027

| Provision | Description | Status |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Annex III (Standalone)** | High-Risk AI Systems (standalone software) | ⏳ PENDING |
| **Art. 6(2)** | High-Risk Classification for specified domains | ⏳ PENDING |
| **Full High-Risk Requirements** | Arts. 9-15 for Annex III systems | ⏳ PENDING |

**Affected Domains (Annex III):**
1. Biometrics (remote identification, emotion recognition in workplace/education)
2. Critical Infrastructure (road traffic, energy, water, digital infrastructure)
3. Education & Vocational Training (admissions, assessments, proctoring)
4. Employment & Worker Management (recruitment, promotion, termination, task allocation)
5. Access to Essential Services (credit scoring, insurance, social benefits)
6. Migration, Asylum & Border Control
7. Justice & Democratic Processes (court evidence, voting, public opinion analysis)

**Preparation Required:**
- Inventory all AI systems used in these domains
- Begin risk management system implementation
- Establish human oversight procedures
- Prepare technical documentation per Annex IV

## ⚠️ Digital Omnibus Changes (Regulation 2026/1744)

The Digital Omnibus Regulation, entering into force 27 July 2026, modified:

### AI Literacy (Art. 4)
- **Before:** Specific "sufficient level" of AI literacy required
- **After:** Simplified obligation - promote AI literacy appropriate to context
- **Effect:** More flexibility, but still mandatory for personnel using AI

### High-Risk Timelines
- **Annex III standalone systems:** Delayed to 2 December 2027 (was 2026)
- **Annex I product integration:** Delayed to 2 August 2028 (was 2027)
- **Rationale:** Allow more time for harmonised standards development

### Enforcement Coordination
- Enhanced AI Office powers at EU level
- Coordinated enforcement approach across Member States
- Single market consistency mechanisms strengthened
